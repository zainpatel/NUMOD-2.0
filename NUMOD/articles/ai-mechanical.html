Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Mechanical Engineering: A Scientific Literature Review on Emerging Paradigms

Author
Md. Irfanul Haque Siddiqui

Published

03 October 2025

DOI

10.36297/vw.jei.v7i4.101

Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) have transformed modern mechanical engineering by enabling intelligent manufacturing, predictive maintenance, physics-informed simulations, optimization, robotics, and autonomous systems. This literature review examines the latest developments in AI-driven mechanical engineering, highlighting current achievements, limitations, and future research directions while emphasizing the importance of trustworthy and explainable AI for engineering applications.

Keywords

Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Mechanical Engineering, Deep Learning, Predictive Maintenance, Generative Design, Physics-Informed Neural Networks

Citation
